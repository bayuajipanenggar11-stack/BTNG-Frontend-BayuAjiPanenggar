BRAND PHILOSOPHY & STORY

The Digital Showroom Reimagined.

VELOCITY was conceived as a digital exploration of automotive prestige — an online showroom where engineering discipline meets editorial clarity.

Rather than overwhelming visitors with noisy commercial banners or inflated specifications, VELOCITY prioritizes intentional typography, precise specifications, and authentic photographic provenance. Every vehicle in this showcase reflects a living record pulled directly from the BTNG API registry.

Designed and built for the BTNG Frontend final project, this platform demonstrates how vanilla web standards — HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript — can produce a refined, responsive, and resilient digital experience without the overhead of heavy third-party frameworks.

ACADEMIC SHOWCASE

Project Scope: VELOCITY is an educational frontend project developed for the BTNG program. It serves as a concept digital showroom powered by the official BTNG API endpoint. It does not represent a commercial dealership or authorized vehicle retailer.

01

Curated Precision

From electric silhouettes to turbocharged grand tourers, each vehicle is presented with authenticated data: year, transmission, fuel architecture, and pricing.

03

Editorial Restraint
